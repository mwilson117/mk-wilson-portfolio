# mk-wilson-portfolio
My personal portfolio website for my Business IT course. This project includes accessibility improvements, visual design principles, and an accessible contact form.
## Accessibility Improvements

- I ran WAVE accessibility checks on my Home, Projects, and About/Contact pages. The initial scans showed zero errors, zero contrast errors, and zero alerts.
- I added a contact form with labels for each input field so users can clearly understand what information to enter.
- I used a fieldset and legend to group the contact information together.
- I added error messages that explain when a required field is missing or an email address is invalid.
- I made the form move keyboard focus to the first field that needs correcting after submission.

## Gestalt Principles

**Proximity:** I grouped related information into sections and kept my contact form labels close to their input fields. This helps users understand which information belongs together.

**Similarity:** I used matching colors, fonts, headings, and navigation across my website's three pages. This makes the design consistent and easier for visitors to understand.

## Visual Design

I used a blue-and-white color scheme throughout my portfolio to keep the design organized and consistent. I also used spacing between sections to make the information easier to read.
